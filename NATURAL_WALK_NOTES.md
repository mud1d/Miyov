# Full-body walking frames, v0.6

Built-in image generation produced 24 full-body poses using yurish_01.png as a character/style reference. The atlas was segmented into 24 alpha components, uniformly scaled by 0.82 and aligned at the right edge and ground baseline in 256px cells. The final 6x4 PNG is quantized to 128 RGBA palette colors for the small overlay. It replaces the articulated-leg renderer for walking. No duplicated PNGs or cross-fading is used to inflate the frame count.

The frames are visually distinct, but generative artwork can still have anatomy or temporal consistency issues. Actual Android playback needs user review. Faster movement reuses this walking sequence; this is not a separate run cycle. Sound is unchanged from 0.5.
