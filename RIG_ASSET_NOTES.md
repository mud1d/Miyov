# Walking puppet assets
Created with built-in image generation using the existing orange kitten reference. Six parts were extracted from the atlas, preserving alpha, and resized for Android. Walking is rendered by the app's inverse-kinematics code with planted stance feet, not by morphing generated full-body images.

Generation prompt:
Use case: precise-object-edit, animation puppet parts extraction. Reference is the character identity: fluffy orange tabby kitten, cream chin/belly and white paws, large dark brown eyes, softly rendered 3D plush look. Create one high-resolution transparent PNG asset atlas, EXACTLY THREE COLUMNS BY TWO ROWS, six isolated animation pieces, generously separated with empty transparent gutters, no text or labels or grid. Do NOT draw six complete cats. Every piece is side-view oriented facing RIGHT. Cell (row1,col1): the complete continuous kitten TORSO with NECK and HEAD and EARS attached, but absolutely NO LEGS, NO PAWS and NO TAIL; lower belly is a smooth intact rounded fur silhouette, not a straight crop. Body is horizontal, head looking forward, face proportions exactly like reference, no floor shadow. Cell (row1,col2): ONLY the fluffy curved orange TAIL with cream tip, detached, its attachment/root at lower right and fluffy tip curling upward left. Cell (row1,col3): ONLY a fluffy orange FRONT UPPER LEG (shoulder to wrist), a vertical short tapered fur segment, round joint ends, NO paw. Cell (row2,col1): ONLY a small LOWER FRONT LEG ending in a complete cream white PAW with small rounded toes facing right, oriented vertically, rounded orange fur upper attachment. Cell (row2,col2): ONLY fluffy orange HIND UPPER LEG (hip/thigh to hock), vertical curved rounded segment with thicker upper thigh, NO paw. Cell (row2,col3): ONLY LOWER HIND LEG with full cream white PAW, vertical, toes pointing right, round orange fur upper attachment. The four leg parts must have no hard cut lines: softly rounded overlapping furry joints for assembly. These are six separated game puppet components for an articulated walking kitten, never a sprite sheet of full cats. Keep exact same fur style and coat colors across all six components. Each component centered inside its cell and using less than 70 percent cell width/height. Absolutely clean true alpha, no gray/checkered rectangles, no shadows, no floating fragments.
