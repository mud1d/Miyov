# Guided walk, v0.7

The v0.6 atlas was visually reviewed on an Android screen recording: paw travel was insufficient and body shapes varied. A first unguided eight-key-pose generation also failed the requested leg order and was rejected. The accepted approach uses a mathematical 4-leg pose guide and a separate character style reference. Eight guided key poses were checked, followed by 32 guided full-body frames. No extra frames were created by file duplication.

Guide: 32 uniformly spaced phases. Stance duty 0.62; stance foot moves +36 to -36 sprite units; swing foot moves -36 to +36 with 30-unit sine lift. Offsets near hind0, near fore0.25, far hind0.5, far fore0.75. Guide geometry is precise but generative rendered poses are approximate. Ground baseline normalization does not guarantee perfect paw planting.

The generated source has 32 disconnected alpha components in8x4 cells. Extracted uniformly at1.12scale, aligned at right edge230 and baseline224 in256px frames; combined into2048x1024 RGBA PNG, quantized192 colors. Alpha below8 removed; nearby feathered alpha assigned to nearest opaque component, avoiding neighboring sprites. All32 decoded frames are unique and have clear corners. The Android viewer plays actual PNG poses, not separate limb rotation. Cycle distance changed32dp→28dp to better match the wider foot travel. Sound, tap states and sleep schedule unchanged. Old atlas retained as historical asset, not loaded for walking.

The preview reconstructs distance indexing at60Hz and30dp/s, with a ground line. It is not an Android device recording. Actual naturalness and residual sliding need on-device feedback; no claim of perfect anatomy or heard audio.

All four legs remain attached anatomically. Preserve the guide's longer visible legs so footfalls can be seen; do not shorten all legs to hidden stubs. Keep the same head/torso silhouette and same joint positions across frames. Side view facing RIGHT. Cute big eyes and plush orange tabby fur/white muzzle like image2, but match image1's CAMERA SIDE VIEW. All eight full-body characters in exactly the same 4x2 grid and placement as image1, no clipping. Image1 white backdrop must become real transparent alpha; no floor, no shadows, no text, no lines, no grid borders. Output 1536x1024. Render only the cats. This animation sheet must follow guide poses over aesthetic pose variation.
